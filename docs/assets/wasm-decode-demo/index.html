ac3forge - WASM decode demo

A real AC-3/E-AC-3 decoder, compiled to WebAssembly and running entirely in this tab: no server-side decode, no upload. Load the bundled Atmos-in-DD+ stream below, or drop in your own .ec3/.ac3 elementary stream.

Solid ring: ear-level channels. Dashed ring: ceiling channels (only lights up for a 7.1.4-class stream). Drag the slider above to scrub. Dot size/glow and the yellow arrow are real per-channel energy from the decode, not object positions - see below.

Real decode, real audio, real per-channel energy - all genuinely out of ac3::forge's WASM build. Not real: individual Atmos object positions. ac3::forge can't read OAMD/JOC back out of a stream yet (encoder-only today), so there's nothing object-level to show.

Part of ac3forge, roadmap item F3. Source: examples/wasm_decode_demo/.
