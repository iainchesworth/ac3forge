ac3forge - WASM decode demo

A real AC-3/E-AC-3 decoder, compiled to WebAssembly and running entirely in this tab: no server-side decode, no upload. Load the bundled Atmos-in-DD+ stream below, or drop in your own .ec3/.ac3 elementary stream.

Real decoded Atmos object positions (OAMD) - top-down and side-on, same layout as the desktop GUI's Objects tab. Dot size is that object's own real audio level (JOC).

Solo an object to hear its own real isolated audio - not its panned slice of the bed.

Solid ring: ear-level channels. Dashed ring: ceiling channels (only lights up for a 7.1.4-class stream). Drag the slider above to scrub. Dot size/glow and the yellow arrow are real per-channel bed energy from the decode.

Everything on this page is real: the decode, the audio, the bed's per-channel energy, and - for a stream with objects - each object's real decoded position (OAMD, ac3::forge#168) and its own real isolated audio (JOC, ac3::forge#169). Nothing here is a canned animation or a re-panned approximation.

Part of ac3forge, roadmap item F3. Source: platform/wasm/.
